Phoenix to Puerto Peñasco (Rocky Point)

Arizona's beach: south through Ajo and Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument, across at Lukeville and Sonoyta, and down to the Sea of Cortez.

  • About 215 mi345 km
  • About 4 hplus the border
  • 1 day
  • US and MexicoArizona and Sonora

Before you go

  • Passport book or card for everyone to get back into the US. US citizens under 16 can use a birth certificate at land crossings.
  • Mexican car insurance. US policies generally don't cover you in Mexico. Buy a Mexican policy before you cross.
  • No vehicle permit needed. Puerto Peñasco is inside the Sonora free zone.
  • Tourist permit (FMM): the rules for short trips have changed over the years. Check with INM (Mexico immigration) or at the office in Sonoyta.
  • Rental car? Make sure the company allows driving into Mexico.
  • Lukeville isn't open 24 hours. Check the CBP Lukeville page for current hours.

The route

  1. Phoenix to Ajo

    About 110 mi, 2 h

    • I-10 west to exit 112, then AZ-85 south through Gila Bend to Ajo.
    • Ajo's Spanish-style plaza is a good break.

  2. Ajo to Lukeville

    About 37 mi, 45 min

    • Through Why and Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument. The visitor center is just off AZ-85.
    • Fill up in Ajo or Why before the border.

  3. Sonoyta to Puerto Peñasco

    About 62 mi, 1 h

    • Cross at Lukeville into Sonoyta, then take Mexico Highway 8 south across the desert to the coast.
    • Pesos and dollars are both widely used in Rocky Point.

Good to know

  • Coming home: lines at Lukeville get long on Sunday afternoons and after holiday weekends. Check the live border wait times before you leave.
  • There's a Border Patrol checkpoint on AZ-85 northbound. Have ID ready.
  • Two cars? Cross together and agree where to meet in Sonoyta in case one gets pulled aside for inspection.
