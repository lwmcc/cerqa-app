Common Questions

How do I delete my account?
Open the Cerqa app, tap Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Account. You can also visit our account deletion page for step-by-step instructions or to request deletion by email.

How do I download my data?
Visit our data download page for information on how to export your Cerqa data.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
On the login screen, tap Forgot Password and follow the instructions sent to your email address.

My account was suspended. What do I do?
If you believe your account was suspended in error, contact us at support@cerqa.net with your username and a description of the issue.

I found a bug or the app is crashing. How do I report it?
Email support@cerqa.net with a description of what happened, the device you are using, and the app version. Screenshots or screen recordings are helpful.
