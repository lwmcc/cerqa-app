How to share your location between iPhone and Android

November 1, 2026 · 2 min read · ES

Most families and groups of friends use a mix of smartphones, with some members using iPhones and others using Android devices. That matters when you want to see where someone is, because the location sharing built into each phone mostly works only with the same type of device.

Here's how to share locations seamlessly between iPhone and Android, and get everyone set up in just a couple of minutes.

Why your phone's built-in location sharing isn't enough

Location sharing built into your phone is typically designed to work best within the same ecosystem. iPhone users can easily share with other iPhone users, and Android users with other Android users. But when your family or friends use a mix of both, staying connected isn't so simple.

To share between an iPhone and an Android phone, you need an app that runs on both. Cerqa is free for iPhone and for Android, and it's made for getting people to the same place: share with one friend or a whole group, see when everyone will arrive, and navigate together.

How to share your location with Cerqa

  1. Both of you install Cerqa from the App Store or Google Play and sign in.
  2. Add each other as connections. You can send an invite link, or scan the other person's QR code when you're together.
  3. On the map, tap Share location, pick your friend or a group, and choose how long to share.
  4. Tap Send request. When they accept, you both see each other live on the map, with how far away they are and when they'll get there.

Sharing ends on its own when the time is up, and you can stop location sharing any time. There's no tracking in the background after that.

Sharing with a group

For a road trip, a game, or a night out, make a group and share with everyone at once. Everyone shows up on one map, iPhones and Android phones together, and the group chat is right there too. On the free plan a group can have up to 5 people and you can share for up to an hour. Pro raises that to 25 people and 24 hours.

Tips

  • Share for a set time, not forever. Most of the time you only need to see someone until they arrive.
  • Turn on precise location for the app on both phones, so the dot is where they really are.
  • Allow location while the app isn't open if you want your friends to keep seeing you when your phone is in your pocket.

Questions
