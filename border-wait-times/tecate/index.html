About this crossing

Tecate is a smaller crossing about 30 miles east of Otay Mesa, between Tecate, Baja California and Tecate, California. It isn't open overnight, so check the hours below before you go.

When Tijuana crossings are busy, the drive to Tecate can be worth it.

Which lane can I use?

  • General lanes are open to everyone.
  • SENTRI lanes are only for trusted travelers. Everyone in the car needs a SENTRI card.
  • Ready Lane is for travelers whose documents have an RFID chip, such as a passport card, a newer Border Crossing Card or an enhanced driver's license. Everyone in the car needs one.
