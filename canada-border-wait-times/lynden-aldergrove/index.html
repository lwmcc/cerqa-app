About this crossing

Lynden-Aldergrove is a smaller crossing east of Blaine, between Washington State Route 539 and BC Highway 13. It isn't open overnight, so check the hours above.

CBSA doesn't publish a wait into Canada for Aldergrove; the other crossings are listed below.

Which lane can I use into the US?

  • General lanes are open to everyone.
  • NEXUS lanes are only for NEXUS members. Everyone in the car needs a NEXUS card.
  • Ready Lane is for travelers whose documents have an RFID chip, such as a passport card, a NEXUS card or an enhanced driver's license. Everyone in the car needs one.

CBSA reports the wait for travelers into Canada as one number per crossing.
