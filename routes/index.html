  • Phoenix to San Diego on I-8About 355 mi, 1 day. The desert way to the coast: from the Valley across the Sonoran Desert, past sand dunes and over the mountains to the Pacific.
  • Grand Canyon loop from PhoenixAbout 640 mi, 4 days. Red rocks, the canyon and slot canyons in one loop: north through Sedona, along the South Rim, up to Page and back down to the Valley.

  • Tijuana to Playa del Carmen by carAbout 2,700 mi, 7 days. Coast to coast across Mexico: the Sonoran Desert, the Pacific in Mazatlán, the highlands around Guadalajara and Puebla, then the Gulf and the Caribbean.
